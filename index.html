Expected Outcome & K-Factor Scaling

The system computes expected scores based on the opponent squad's average MMR versus the player's personal rating:

Expected_Score = 1 / (1 + 10^((Opponent_Avg_MMR - Current_MMR) / 400))
↳ K-Factor 40 for newcomers (< 15 games) for fast calibration.
↳ K-Factor 28 for seasoned veterans for rating stability.
