The Mathematical MMR Architecture

An esports-grade algorithm calibrated to reward skill, squad leadership, and class versatility.

Expected Outcome & K-Factor Scaling

Individual score deltas are determined by computing personal rating versus opposing squad average rating:

Expected_Score = 1 / (1 + 10^((Opponent_Avg_MMR - Current_MMR) / 400))
↳ K-Factor 40 for newcomers (< 15 matches) for fast rank calibration.
↳ K-Factor 28 for seasoned veterans for leaderboard stabilization.
